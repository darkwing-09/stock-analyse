# Surface: dashboard shell (src/App.tsx and the four views)

Mode: Operate. The visitor opens this after the close to answer, in under a
minute: how is the paper portfolio doing, what fills tomorrow, is the strategy
trustworthy, what did the market do today.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quant desk lit from one source. The page refuses the category default
of a grey admin grid with an orange accent and equal-size metric cards; instead
one cool light (spotlight beams, pointer-tracked glow, an orbiting point on the
live chip) is the whole brand, and data sits in tabular Geist on near-black.

OWN-WORLD: ground `#080808`, panels `#0f0f0f` with `white/8` hairlines, text
`#ededed / #a1a1aa / #8b8b8b`, accent `#3b82f6` only where something is active,
focused or lit; series blue vs dashed orange; status red/green on icons alone.
With all content removed it still reads as a dark desk under a single lamp.

STORY: "My simulated money is here, tomorrow's orders are those, the strategy
beat Nifty but only modestly and not lately, and the market is risk-on." The
visitor runs the evening command, reloads, and trusts the numbers because the
benchmark and the caveats sit beside them.

FIRST VIEWPORT (laptop): sticky bar with brand, four pill tabs and the live
regime chip; the title resolves out of a blur; a wide equity panel (headline
number, return chip, four stats, the equity line against Nifty) beside a
narrower "Next session" order list; open positions below the fold.

FORM: standard dashboard shell (top bar, tab views, panels, sortable tables) in
the committed palette. seed: none (pinned) — "use the websites I gave you as
resource (animmasterlib.dev, ui.aceternity.com, skiper-ui.com) … check the
animations, transitions and colors from color palette, I want sleek and decent
website".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.
